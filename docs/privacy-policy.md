# Privacy policy for Marriage – Nepali Card Game

*Last updated: 13 October 2026*

Marriage is a card game made by Mohit Dhamala. This page explains what the
app does with information about you: in short, it sends crash reports and a
few anonymous game statistics to help fix and improve it, and nothing that
says who you are.

## What the app collects

The app has no accounts and no ads. On Android it uses two Google Firebase
services:

- **Firebase Crashlytics** sends a report when the app crashes or hits an
  error: what went wrong in the code, the device model, Android version and
  app version, and a random ID for this installation.
- **Google Analytics for Firebase** records which screens are opened and a
  few game events: a game starting (its scoring mode, stake table and bot
  difficulty), a round finishing (won or lost, and whether you had seen the
  joker), and leaving a game part-way through. Analytics also records basic
  device and usage information, such as the device model, the country and a
  random ID for this installation.

None of this includes your player name, your coins, your cards, or anything
you type. It's used only to find and fix bugs and to see which parts of the
game are played. It is processed by Google under
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), and the app
sells and shares none of it.

## What stays on your device

Your player name, avatar colour, settings and play-money coin balance are
saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. Uninstalling the app
deletes them.

## Playing with friends

When you host or join a game over Wi-Fi or a phone's hotspot, your player
name and your moves are sent directly between the phones at that table, on
your local network. They don't pass through the internet, and nothing is
kept once the game ends. The app asks for network access for this and for
the reports above.

## Children

The app is not designed for children and doesn't knowingly collect
information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
