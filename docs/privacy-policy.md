# Privacy policy for Marriage – Nepali Card Game

*Last updated: 10 October 2026*

Marriage is a card game made by Mohit Dhamala. This page explains what the
app does with information about you: in short, it collects nothing.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and
it never sends anything to the internet or to any server.

## What stays on your device

Your player name, avatar colour, settings and play-money coin balance are
saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. Uninstalling the app
deletes them.

## Playing with friends

When you host or join a game over Wi-Fi or a phone's hotspot, your player
name and your moves are sent directly between the phones at that table, on
your local network. Nothing passes through the internet, and nothing is kept
once the game ends. This is the only reason the app asks for network access.

## Children

The app is not designed for children and doesn't knowingly collect anything
from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
